Learning systems · Scientific discovery

My research asks how models with different structures and capabilities can exchange knowledge—and how understanding that process can make AI systems more reliable, interpretable, and useful for science.

Current project

Heterogeneous model merging

Most model-merging methods assume that the models being combined share the same architecture. I am exploring the harder setting: transferring and integrating capabilities across heterogeneous models while preserving what each model does well.

Question 01

Which representations remain compatible across different architectures?

Question 02

How can we measure successful knowledge transfer beyond aggregate accuracy?

Question 03

What does merging reveal about where models store reusable capabilities?
