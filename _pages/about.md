Hi, I am **Ziheng Zhao**.

I am an undergraduate student in the [School of Statistics](http://stat.ruc.edu.cn/) at Renmin University of China, majoring in **Applied Economics and Data Science**. I am currently supervised by [Prof. Hongteng Xu](https://hongtengxu.github.io/) in the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence.

My research interests lie in **artificial intelligence**, with a current focus on **heterogeneous model merging**. More broadly, I am interested in model architecture design, LLM interpretability, and AI for Science.

I also have training in applied economics and am especially interested in **game theory** and **economic growth theory**, connecting economic reasoning with data-driven and machine-learning methods.

## Research Interests

- Heterogeneous model merging
- LLM architecture and interpretability
- AI for Science
- Game theory and economic growth

- UC Berkeley Data 100 — Principles and Techniques of Data Science
